# Bylaws long-form visualizations

All assets are 1536 × 1024 PNGs (3:2 landscape) generated as independent editorial inserts. Source of truth: `app/content/bylaws.ts`.

## 01-good-standing.png

- **Source section:** Article III §2, “Good Standing.”
- **Placement:** After the Article III §2 section content and before Article III §3, “Dues.”
- **Concept:** Four joint good-standing conditions feed four member rights; a separate public-supporter lane shows participation without governance rights.
- **Labels used:** “GOOD STANDING”; “Dues paid + 30-day grace”; “1 regular meeting / year”; “Code of Conduct”; “Live, work, study, or worship in San Joaquin County”; “Vote”; “Hold office”; “Serve on committees”; “Propose business”; “PUBLIC SUPPORTERS”; “Participate”; “No vote”; “No office”; “No committee chair.”
- **Qualification encoded:** Public supporters may participate but cannot vote, hold office, or chair a committee; all four good-standing conditions are presented together.

## 02-quorum-calculation.png

- **Source section:** Article IV §3, “Quorum,” especially §§3.1–3.5.
- **Placement:** After the Article IV §3 section content and before Article IV §4, “Notification of Meetings.”
- **Concept:** Three large, mobile-legible bands show meeting eligibility, the five-step quorum formula, and the current-meeting branch that merges before enforcing the vote threshold.
- **Labels used:** “ELIGIBLE MEETING”; “Official minutes + attendance taken”; “Start + each vote”; “Single-meeting average”; “Average 6 meetings”; “50%”; “Round up”; “Attendance changed?”; “YES · Recheck”; “NO · Use confirmed quorum”; “At or above quorum → VOTE”; “Below quorum → NO VOTE.”
- **Qualification encoded:** Only meetings with official minutes recorded and attendance taken feed the six-meeting calculation. Changed attendance triggers a recheck; unchanged attendance uses the previously confirmed quorum. Both branches still prohibit voting below quorum.

## 04-election-path.png

- **Source section:** Article V §§2–3, “Nominations Committee” and “Voting.”
- **Placement:** After Article V §3 and before Article VI, “Steering Committee.”
- **Concept:** Election preparation flows into valid-vote majority testing. A first-ballot winner and the outcome of any same-meeting runoff independently feed one global election-recordkeeping footer.
- **Labels used:** “2-member Nominations Committee”; “At least 2 General Meetings before election”; “VALID VOTES”; “Abstentions excluded”; “More than half?”; “YES”; “WINNER”; “NO”; “Same-meeting runoff”; “Top tied candidates”; “RUNOFF OUTCOME”; “ALL ELECTION VOTES”; “Named voting records”; “Member inspection.”
- **Qualification encoded:** Abstentions are excluded; winning requires more than half of valid votes; the runoff is limited to the top tied candidates; named, inspectable records apply to all election votes, not only a runoff.

## 08-focus-campaign-lifecycle.png

- **Source section:** Article VII §§1–3.
- **Placement:** After Article VII §3, “Campaign Operation,” and before Article VII §4, “Campaign Privileges.”
- **Concept:** A one-way initial path runs from advance notice through two-thirds adoption, a leaderless first month, and the one-month leadership election into a continuing campaign. A separate six-month renewal loop returns only to the continuing campaign.
- **Labels used:** “MAX 2 ACTIVE”; “INITIAL PATH”; “Proposal announced 1 General Meeting ahead”; “2/3 adoption”; “Initial budget: majority vote”; “FIRST MONTH”; “No formal leadership”; “Campaigns Coordinator: basic administration”; “ONE MONTH LATER”; “Leadership elected: simple majority”; “CONTINUING CAMPAIGN”; “EVERY 6 MONTHS”; “Reapproval + new leadership election”; “Simple majority.”
- **Qualification encoded:** The initial budget majority vote attaches only to initial adoption. Renewal and a new leadership election occur together every six months by simple majority and loop directly back to the continuing campaign, without repeating advance notice or two-thirds adoption.

## 09-side-quest-decision-tree.png

- **Source section:** Article VIII introductory text and §§1–2.
- **Placement:** After Article VIII §2, “Democratic Approval of Side-Quests,” and before Article IX, “Committees.”
- **Concept:** Four large, mobile-legible bands show the proposal, membership’s campaign-suitability question, two isolated answer columns, and approval choices confined to the no/continue branch.
- **Labels used:** “ANY MEMBER PROPOSES”; “Limited scope · Outside 2-campaign cap”; “MEMBERSHIP”; “Better suited to a campaign?”; “YES”; “Authorize 2-member campaign-development group”; “OR END”; “NO · CONTINUE AS SIDE-QUEST”; “APPROVAL”; “General Meeting · simple majority”; “OR”; “URGENT BETWEEN MEETINGS”; “Quorum + 1 or 3 · whichever is higher”; “Steering votes · less than half.”
- **Qualification encoded:** Only the no/continue branch reaches Side-Quest approval. The urgent threshold uses whichever number is higher, and Steering members must be fewer than half of the approving votes.

## 10-bylaw-amendment-path.png

- **Source section:** Article X §3, “Changes to the Bylaws,” including §3.1.
- **Placement:** After Article X §3 and before Article X §4, “Compliance with IRS Guidelines and State Regulations.”
- **Concept:** After required notice, an unclassified amendment stays with the current proposal. If Steering classifies it as separate, membership decides whether to overrule: yes rejoins the current proposal, while no sends the amendment through a separate standard bylaw-change process.
- **Labels used:** “BYLAW CHANGE”; “Announce 1 meeting before vote”; “Member amendments”; “Announce amendments 2 weeks before vote”; “Steering classifies amendment as separate?”; “YES”; “NO”; “Membership overrules classification?”; “YES: simple majority before final vote”; “Separate standard bylaw-change process”; “CURRENT PROPOSAL”; “FINAL ADOPTION”; “2/3 of members present.”
- **Qualification encoded:** Steering classifies but does not veto. An overrule by simple majority returns the amendment to the current proposal’s final two-thirds vote; no overrule starts a separate bylaw-change process and does not enter the current final vote.
