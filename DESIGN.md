# Working Class Unity: Open Assembly

This is the agent-readable design authority for Working Class Unity. Load it before shaping, designing,
implementing, reviewing, or materially changing any WCU surface that affects what a visitor or member
sees, understands, chooses, or does.

The selected direction is **Open Assembly**: a living public record of collective work made from open
fields, large direct statements, documentary proof, dated source trails, and clear ways to participate.
It should feel like an organizing handbill that learned excellent civic information design—not a
nonprofit brochure, campaign template, government portal, or software landing page.

Internal test: **Clear enough to trust. Human enough to join. Forceful enough to organize.** This is a
design test, not public copy.

## Authority

When sources compete, use this order:

1. Harpreet's explicit instruction and accepted redesign decisions.
2. Verified organizational records, bylaws, campaign sources, current operations, and approved content.
3. This file for design judgment, composition, voice, and public identity.
4. Repository tokens and component contracts for exact mechanics.
5. Approved exemplars and frozen evaluation fixtures.
6. Existing WCU code and published pages as evidence, not automatic precedent.
7. External references and generic design heuristics.

Do not let design imply an organizational fact, campaign claim, current status, data policy, legal
promise, member eligibility rule, or publication permission that its canonical owner does not support.

## Visitor job and success

Within about 20 seconds, a newcomer should be able to identify that:

1. WCU is a real, local, member-run organization.
2. Members act together; WCU is not an individual-service nonprofit.
3. WCU is doing concrete work now and can show honest results.
4. A newcomer can begin with a low-pressure human interaction.
5. Membership is a meaningful later commitment, not a checkout funnel.

The site's organizational outcomes are increased event attendance and increased new membership. Other
analytics are diagnostic inputs, not design goals.

## Composition

### Compose a field, not a stack of boxes

Treat a page as one continuous canvas. Establish relationships with scale, alignment, density,
whitespace, contrast fields, and horizontal rules before adding a container.

A surface or border must communicate at least one of these:

- a real interaction or selection;
- an urgent organizing signal;
- a warning, error, or disclosure;
- a meaningful contrast field;
- a grouping that spacing and alignment cannot express clearly.

Do not use a card merely because content belongs together. Do not put cards inside cards. Do not make
every section the same silhouette.

### Use one throughline

Each page has one primary job and one visually dominant next action. Adapt this sequence to the job:

> Context → claim → proof → next step

- **Context:** authorship, place, status, and current-through date.
- **Claim:** the plain point WCU needs the visitor to understand.
- **Proof:** a result, activity, governance mechanism, record, source, event, or approved image.
- **Next step:** one realistic action and what happens after it.

Keep evidence close to the claim. Support both a quick path—title, headings, facts, captions, next
action—and an audit path with dates, sources, qualifications, and privacy boundaries.

### Match the opening to the page

- Identity: who WCU is and why member self-government matters.
- Current Work: currentness, status, objective, evidence, participation.
- Campaign: supported claim and decisive public record as co-primary.
- Events: date, access, place, type, and accurate RSVP state.
- Get Involved: a low-pressure first step, not dues or checkout.
- Tenant Rights: usable, current information and its limits.
- Voices: the publication's claim or event, not a generic masthead.
- Member product: the task and state, not campaign styling.

Open Assembly is a system, not a universal page template.

## Two registers

**Foundation** is the default: canvas, white, ink, navy, Acumin Pro, narrow readable prose, quiet rules,
dates and sources, little or no texture, and no decorative motion.

**Organizing/editorial** appears only when pressure is truthful: active campaigns, actions, selected
event moments, Voices features, and historical packages. Use one strong red, orange, navy, or ink field;
a short Akira Jimbo declaration; asymmetry, meaningful data or sequence numerals, or a restrained print device; and keep claim,
proof, status/source, and action together. Forms, logistics, legal language, payments, and long reading
stay calm. An ordinary page gets at most one dominant organizing moment.

## Brand anchors

| Role           | Value     | Use                                                              |
| -------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Navy           | `#04334f` | institutional anchor, headings, shell, stable actions            |
| Red            | `#ef2525` | large organizing accents and display moments                     |
| Accessible red | `#ae0000` | urgent controls or fields carrying normal white text             |
| Orange         | `#ff9f48` | annotation, community warmth, meaningful numerals with dark text |
| Canvas         | `#f7f9fc` | public page ground                                               |
| Ink            | `#232323` | body text and calm dark fields                                   |
| White          | `#ffffff` | primary surface and text on dark fields                          |
| Rule           | `#cbd4dc` | quiet structural dividers                                        |
| Muted text     | `#5f6f79` | legible secondary information                                    |
| Placeholder    | `#e6ebef` | neutral media placeholders                                       |

Canvas, white, ink, and navy do most of the work. Red and orange are punctuation. Orange always uses a
dark foreground. Raw red never carries normal-size white text. Color never communicates state alone.
No decorative gradients, gradient text, glows, glass, or multicolor backgrounds.

## Typography

- **Acumin Pro Regular and Bold:** body, navigation, buttons, forms, data, captions, articles, and most headings.
- **Akira Jimbo:** a short identity, campaign, or editorial declaration only—roughly one to seven words and no more than two lines.
- **Fallback sans serif:** required when a supplied face lacks a Spanish or Punjabi glyph or reduces readability.

Use 12–14px metadata, 16px minimum body/UI, 18–20px ledes, approximately 28px small headings,
32–44px section turns, and 44–72px page displays. Body leading is about 1.55 and reading measure is
60–72ch. Sentence case is the default. Short uppercase is reserved for real status, place, or
currentness labels. Never shrink a translation to preserve a composition; let the layout grow or
recompose.

### Numerals must carry meaning

Do not use decorative ordinal numbers for parallel choices, arguments, FAQ entries, headings, cards,
or page-outline entries. Use numbers only for verified quantities, source references, formal document
structure, or a genuinely ordered sequence such as instructions, a timeline, or canonical numbered
demands. If items can be rearranged without changing their meaning, do not number them.

## Grid, spacing, and geometry

- Desktop reference: 12 columns in a 1248px content field on a 1440px canvas.
- Tablet reference: 8 columns with at least 32px outer gutters.
- Mobile reference: 4 columns with 24px gutters; remain usable at 320px.
- Preferred claim/proof relationships: asymmetric 7/5 or 4/8 splits.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px.
- Major section turns: about 96px desktop and 64px mobile.
- Default radius: square to 4px; large non-overlay surfaces never exceed 8px.
- Structural rules: 1px; deliberate emphasis: 4px.
- Shadows: only true floating overlays such as menus and dialogs.
- Pills: compact filters, toggles, and categorical states only.

Every object aligns to a shared edge, baseline, grid line, or deliberate optical center. Empty space must
amplify something; an accidental empty rectangle is not minimalism.

## Logo and media

Use the full circular WCU emblem as the primary signature and the compact mark only where the full seal
cannot remain legible. Use supplied dark-on-light and light-on-dark files only. Keep at least one outer
red dot's diameter of clear space around the seal. Do not redraw, recolor, crop, rotate, stretch, outline,
shadow, or deconstruct it.

Documentary media is evidence, not atmosphere. Use only approved, consented WCU photography. Until an
image is approved, use a neutral placeholder in the intended 16:9, 4:3, 3:2, 3:4, or 9:16 ratio and
label it as pending—not as evidence. Never generate synthetic people to represent WCU members,
residents, or activity. Captions should identify the useful safe subset of what, place, date, context,
source, and consent status.

## Navigation and actions

Target public destinations are Who We Are, Current Work, Events, Tenant Rights, and Voices. `Get
Involved` is the emphasized action. Language and Member Login are quiet utilities. Do not publish a
navigation item until it has a real destination.

Use literal labels such as `See upcoming events`, `See the campaign`, `Sign the demand letter`, `Read
how membership works`, and `Contact WCU`. Avoid `Learn more` when the destination can be named.

Navy is the default primary action. Accessible red is reserved for genuinely urgent campaign action.
Orange is not a primary control color. Do not show several equal-weight filled actions together.
Navigation uses links; commands use buttons. Touch targets are at least 44×44px, focus is always visible,
and long labels may wrap rather than clip.

## Shared component contract

Use native HTML first, then an existing app component, then a documented Reka primitive for genuinely
composite interaction. Extract components for repeated semantics, behavior, accessibility policy, or a
stable visual contract—not repeated markup alone. Components do not own outer page margins, and their
props express small semantic APIs rather than arbitrary CSS values.

The initial Open Assembly shared surface is:

- `AppButton`: native command button only.
- `AppActionLink`: internal or external navigational action; primary, secondary, or campaign emphasis.
- `EvidenceMetaLine`: supplied status, place, current-through date, and source in a stable order; omit unknowns.
- `DocumentaryFigure`: approved media or an explicit neutral placeholder with provenance-aware captioning.
- `ProofStrip`: maintained facts with label, freshness, source, and context in open cells divided by rules.
- `AppTopbar`: the one public shell header with a native mobile disclosure.
- `AppField`, `AppInput`, `AppNotice`, `PageOutline`, and documented feature-owned Reka integrations.

Do not create generic `AppBox`, `AppCard`, `AppStack`, polymorphic `AppText`, or a link/button hybrid.
Pages own composition and section order. CSS layout primitives may own container, reading measure, flow,
cluster, grid, split, full-bleed field, and divided row operations.

## Voice and content integrity

WCU voice is plainspoken, warm, local working-class identity; patient concrete education; forceful,
evidence-led campaign language; and calm literal transactional, privacy, governance, and error language.

Prefer concrete nouns and verbs, short paragraphs, descriptive headings, and direct explanation of what
happens next. Do not use generic aspiration, inflated certainty, unexplained insider language, startup
conversion language, or nonprofit-service framing. Say what is unknown, provisional, dated, or outside
WCU's role.

Public proof may include total membership only when current and approved. Never publish member lists,
worker/tenant/neighbor details, or member geography/demographics. Do not invent counts, testimonials,
partners, outcomes, dates, urgency, or progress.

## Responsive and accessibility gate

Every completed slice must be checked at desktop, 390px, 320px, keyboard-only navigation, reduced motion,
200% text size, and realistic translated-content expansion. Required outcomes:

- no horizontal page overflow, clipped text, or overlapping controls;
- semantic source order remains logical when columns stack;
- focus is visible and not obscured;
- all interactive targets remain at least 44×44px;
- contrast meets WCAG AA for the text size in use;
- headings, landmarks, names, descriptions, and current-page state remain meaningful;
- placeholders are distinguishable from documentary proof;
- content grows or recomposes instead of shrinking below readable sizes.

Automated accessibility checks are evidence, not proof. Pair them with rendered inspection and keyboard
review. A design is not implemented until its desktop and mobile composition both work.

## First implementation sequence

1. Install this repository trigger and authority.
2. Install the supplied fonts and semantic tokens.
3. Restyle the existing shell and low-level controls; add the navigational-action contract.
4. Add metadata, documentary-figure, and proof-strip contracts.
5. Rebuild Home as the first complete vertical slice and evaluate it at all required extremes.
6. Continue with Current Work, Remove Flock, Get Involved, and remaining launch families, reusing only proven contracts.
